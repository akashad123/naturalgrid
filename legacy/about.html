About our journey

Natural Grid LLP is a Kerala-based renewable-energy company headquartered in Sulthan Bathery, Wayanad. Incorporated as a Limited Liability Partnership, we focus on solar power plant installation, annual maintenance contracts (AMC), servicing, repair, inspection, performance monitoring and complete lifecycle support. Our strength comes from hands-on field experience — we understand what happens to a solar plant after commissioning, and we have built our company around solving exactly that.

We also collaborate with the Kisan Service Society (KSS), a non-profit network with units across Kerala, helping solar awareness and reliable service reach households and farmers even in rural areas — while all technical assessments, quotations and payments are handled directly and only by Natural Grid LLP.

How We Work

However you reach us, every job follows the same five steps — so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

Talk to Us
  1. 01

    You reach out

    Call, message or fill in the form — a real person picks it up. We get back to you within 24 hours and, where practical, arrange a site visit within 24–48 hours.

  2. 02

    We visit, evaluate, and document the best solution

    We look at your roof, wiring and KSEB bill, then give you a clear written plan and quote. We also help with the KSEB and subsidy paperwork where it is needed.

  3. 03

    The right team does the work

    Trained technicians install your system using approved, ISI-compliant materials — with safe, careful workmanship from the first bracket to the final connection.

  4. 04

    We finish and check with you

    Once it is done, we test the system, walk you through how it works, and only close the job when you are happy. Every enquiry is followed up until it is properly wrapped up.

  5. 05

    We stay on with AMC

    After handover we keep in touch. Our AMC plans cover inspection, monitoring and breakdown help — so your plant keeps generating well, year after year.

Care after installation

Your solar plant deserves regular care

Most solar problems don't appear on day one. Dust build-up, wiring wear, inverter faults and monsoon-related issues gradually reduce generation — often without the owner noticing.

Scheduled Inspection

Periodic technical inspections protect generation and catch small issues before they become expensive ones.

Breakdown Assistance

Rapid response for unexpected system faults, including troubleshooting, on-site inspection, repair coordination, spare parts replacement, and warranty support with manufacturers (where applicable) to restore your system quickly.
