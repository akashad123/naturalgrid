Our Services

Battery Backup & Grid Systems

Standard on-grid solar systems shut down during grid failure for safety (anti-islanding). If you need power during outages, a hybrid or off-grid system with batteries is required — and that's exactly what we design and install.

How It Works

01

Load & Backup Assessment

We assess which loads you need running during outages, for how long, and your site's grid reliability — so the battery capacity fits your actual needs.

02

Hybrid or On/Off-Grid Design

Hybrid systems combine solar, grid and battery backup for uninterrupted power; off-grid systems provide independent, battery-based supply where the grid is unreliable or absent ;On-grid systems combine solar and grid power to reduce electricity costs by exporting excess energy to the utility network through net metering.

03

Installation & Support

Quality installation with approved materials, followed by long-term support — including AMC plans that cover batteries, inverters and protection systems.

What We Offer

  • Hybrid solar systems — solar, grid and battery backup combined for uninterrupted power.
  • Off-grid solar systems — independent, battery-based systems for locations without reliable grid supply.
  • On-grid solar systems — A standard on-grid solar system automatically shuts down during a power outage for safety reasons and does not provide backup power
  • Battery backup and UPS inverters — reliable backup power solutions for homes, farms and institutions.
  • Battery additions and upgrades to existing solar installations, where technically feasible.

Design & Installation

  • Battery capacity is sized to your actual backup needs — essential loads, backup duration and site conditions.
  • We use approved, reputed brands and ISI-compliant materials; final make and model depend on technical design, availability and the approved quotation.
  • Installation includes wiring, earthing and protection-system checks with safe workmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my solar work during a power cut?

Standard on-grid systems shut down automatically during grid failure for safety reasons (anti-islanding). If backup power matters to you, we'll design a hybrid or off-grid configuration with the right battery capacity for your essential loads.

Can you add batteries to my existing system?

Yes, where technically feasible — we offer battery additions and upgrades to existing solar installations.
